Hosting & domain

Keep it online.

Hosting is $200 for the first year, then $200 per year to continue. Your domain remains yours. If you already have one, we can connect it to the site. If you need help getting one and setting up business email addresses such as you@yourbusiness.com, that setup is a one-time $50 service.

You pay for the domain and its renewals directly. As part of your hosting package, I will remind you when your domain renewal is coming up and notify you when it is due. The domain provider may offer business email addresses; its pricing and availability depend on the provider.
