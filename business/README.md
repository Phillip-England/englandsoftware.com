## Focus

England Software is a solo development business serving small businesses that want a professional website and do not have an IT department. The primary offer is a simple, affordable website. Hosting is a natural follow-on service. Custom web applications remain available for businesses with a specific operational problem, but they are a secondary, consultative offer.

Phillip's background in finance and operations across two Chick-fil-A locations in Tulsa informs the approach: learn how the business works, then build a useful solution without unnecessary complexity.

## The three offers

| Offer | Who it is for | Starting point |

## Positioning for the website

> Professional websites for small businesses without an IT department. Get a site that explains what you do, shows the services you offer, and gives customers a way to reach you. England Software can also host your site and build custom applications when your business needs more than a website.

Lead with websites. Mention hosting beside the website offer. Present web applications as a separate path for businesses with more complex needs. Remove contract engineering from the public service list.

## Keep the business manageable

- Sell a defined starter website instead of an open-ended design project at the $300 price.
- State what the client must supply, what the site includes, and what changes cost extra before work starts.
- Keep basic hosting limited to a simple website. Quote applications and their hosting separately.
- Avoid promising around-the-clock support or a level of maintenance that one person cannot sustain.
